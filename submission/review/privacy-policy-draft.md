# AgentRAG privacy policy — draft for publisher review

Publisher: Mathieu MOUBRI. Contact: mathieu.moubri@gmail.com.

AgentRAG processes your account email, user identifier, Google identifier/profile information when you choose Google sign-in, uploaded document text and titles, document embeddings, questions and relevant conversation context, OAuth authorizations and tokens, and technical request/error information needed to operate the service.

These data are used to authenticate you, keep documents associated with your account, find relevant passages, generate answers and citations, and investigate service failures. The plugin's tools perform read-only queries within your own knowledge base. Document management takes place on the website.

The site and PostgreSQL database are hosted by MonsterASP.NET in Europe, as confirmed by the publisher. Questions, relevant document passages and embedding inputs are processed by Azure AI Foundry in Sweden, as confirmed by the publisher. Google processes Google sign-in; ChatGPT/Codex process prompts and tool outputs under their respective policies. Hosting, database, model and authentication providers may process data necessary to deliver these services. Account authorizations and requested tool results are returned to the client you connect.

Account information is retained while your account exists. Document text, chunks and embeddings remain until you delete the document or your account is deleted. Deleting a document removes its associated chunks from the active database. The application does not persist chat conversations in its database; the connected client may retain its own history. OAuth access tokens last one hour and refresh tokens last 30 days, with rotation and revocation. Expired token metadata may remain until operational cleanup.

PUBLISHER CHECK REQUIRED: the retention of infrastructure logs, expired OAuth records, backups and disaster-recovery copies has not yet been confirmed for the publisher's Premium hosting plan. The host's public help page describes 14-day backups and up to 31-day disaster-recovery copies, while its Terms describe 21-day Premium backups. Do not publish an asserted deletion deadline until the actual account configuration/contract is checked.

You can delete documents on the website, disconnect a client, revoke Google access from your Google account, and request access, correction or account deletion at the contact address. Identity verification may be needed. Requests are subject to applicable law. Deletion from active storage does not immediately erase existing provider backups. Further provider processing/retention and any international transfers must be reviewed against the actual provider terms before publication.

This draft intentionally contains no claims that data are never used for model training, that every provider copy is erased immediately, or that compliance certification has been obtained. Those statements have not been established.
